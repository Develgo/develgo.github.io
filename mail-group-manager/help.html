Managing Email Contacts

  • You can add, edit, and delete email contacts.
  • Contacts include first name, last name, and email address.

Managing Email Groups

  • You can add, edit, and delete email groups.
  • Email groups consist of multiple email contacts and a description.

Importing and Exporting

  • You can import or export all data including contacts and groups in standard formats.

Copy and Paste Features

  • You can copy an email contact or an entire group and paste it directly into your email client’s To field.
  • You can paste a contact from your email client (e.g., John Turner <john.turner@example.com>;) into the add/edit form to quickly create a new contact.
